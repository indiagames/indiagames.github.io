Refund & Return Policy

Last Updated: September 2, 2026

At Deepak IT & Web Services, all browser-based games and casual web applications hosted on our hub are completely free to access and play. There are no mandatory subscription fees, paywalls, or digital product purchases required to enjoy our content.

1. Voluntary Contributions & Donations

Any optional contributions or support sent via secure third-party platforms (such as PayPal or Razorpay) are strictly voluntary gestures of appreciation from users. Because no digital goods or physical items are sold or delivered in exchange, all voluntary contributions are non-refundable.

2. No Financial Transactions for Content

Since our platform features open-source, free-to-play web entertainment, no monetary charges apply for playing games, browsing pages, or accessing core features. Consequently, no return or refund claims apply.
